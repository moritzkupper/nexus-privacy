# Nexus Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 13, 2026**

## Overview

Nexus is a private personal application used by its owner for productivity, health tracking, fitness tracking, and personal analytics.

Nexus may connect to third-party services such as WHOOP through their official APIs in order to import and analyze personal data authorized by the user.

## WHOOP Data

When connected to WHOOP, Nexus may access data made available through the official WHOOP API, including:

* Recovery data
* Heart rate variability (HRV)
* Resting heart rate
* Sleep data
* Sleep stages
* Strain and physiological cycle data
* Workout and activity data
* Profile information
* Body measurements

Nexus only accesses data that has been explicitly authorized through the WHOOP OAuth consent process.

## Purpose of Processing

WHOOP data is used exclusively for the owner's personal use, including:

* personal health tracking
* historical health trends
* sleep and recovery analysis
* workout and training analysis
* health and performance analytics

## Data Storage

Imported data is stored on private server infrastructure controlled by the owner of Nexus.

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored privately and are used only to maintain the authorized connection with WHOOP and synchronize permitted data.

## Data Sharing

Nexus does not:

* sell personal data
* use personal data for advertising
* share personal data with advertisers
* intentionally provide personal data to unrelated third parties

Nexus may communicate with services that are required for its operation, including WHOOP itself.

## OAuth Credentials

WHOOP OAuth access and refresh tokens are used solely to authenticate with the WHOOP API and synchronize data that the user has authorized Nexus to access.

These credentials are not intentionally disclosed to third parties.

## Data Retention

Imported data may be retained for personal historical analysis until the owner chooses to delete it.

Disconnecting WHOOP or revoking OAuth access does not necessarily delete data that has already been imported into Nexus.

## Revoking Access

WHOOP access can be revoked at any time by:

* disconnecting WHOOP within Nexus
* revoking Nexus access through the user's WHOOP account, where available

After access is revoked, Nexus will no longer be able to retrieve new WHOOP data unless authorization is granted again.
